Indirim Koparan

Indirim Koparan is an AI-powered deal publishing system for the Turkish market. A local pipeline collects discounted products from Amazon, Trendyol, n11 and Hepsiburada, generates Turkish product copy and hashtags with a language model, renders vertical short-form videos automatically, and publishes the result to Telegram, Instagram and YouTube — about 10 products per day.

Built and operated solo since 2026. TikTok publishing is pending platform review. Contact: info@indirimkoparan.com · t.me/indirim_koparan.
